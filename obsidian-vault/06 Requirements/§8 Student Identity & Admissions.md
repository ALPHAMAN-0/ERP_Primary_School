# §8 Student Identity & Admissions

Every student gets a permanent, unique ID at admission, never reused — even after graduation or withdrawal.

Format: `YYMMSSSS` (8 digits) — 2-digit year + 2-digit zero-padded month + 4-digit serial. Example: the 5th student admitted in August 2026 becomes `26080005`. Capacity: up to 9,999 admissions per month.

Open item: a phased rollout (pilot one grade first) was recommended but not explicitly confirmed; currently the working default unless revisited.
